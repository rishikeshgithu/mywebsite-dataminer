About the Founder

P S Rishikesh is a passionate Electronics and Communication Engineer with certifications in full-stack development and machine learning. A NASSCOM-certified developer and Kaggle contributor, Rishi brings a rare blend of hands-on coding expertise and real-world business sense to the table.

Throughout his B.Tech at Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham, he worked with award-winning companies and top-tier research institutes like IISc and IIT Hyderabad, developing practical AI applications in areas ranging from market prediction to autonomous vehicles and healthcare diagnostics.

Rishikesh has interned as an AI engineer at startups, contributed to disruptive innovations, and executed complex projects like a GPT-powered market data pipeline and multi-agent ML systems. His projects span voice AI, computer vision, OCR, IoT, and embedded AI.

With deep exposure to technologies like Python, C++, Raspberry Pi, OpenCV, Streamlit, React Native, and cloud platforms like AWS, he brings full-stack problem-solving firepower to both short-term trade systems and long-term investment tools.

Driven by a mission to empower retail investors, Rishi leads Data Miners with a core belief: that consistent execution beats chaos, and high conviction beats hype. Through one-minute trades, deep research, and practical insights, he’s building a movement of retail wealth creators — one day, one trade at a time.
